### Smart India Hackathon 2026

**Problem Statement ID:** SIH26017

**Problem Statement:** Predictive Analytics System for Early Detection of Land Acquisition Delays

**Theme:** Smart Automation

**Category:** Software

**Team:** LATENCY ZERO

---

## Objective

To develop a predictive analytics system that identifies the likelihood
of land acquisition delays at an early stage and provides risk scores,
visualizations, early warnings, and recommendations.

---

## Proposed Solution

LandPredict uses AI/ML to predict the likelihood of delay and generate
project-based risk scores.

The system combines:

- Machine Learning
- Explainable AI
- GIS
- Risk Prediction
- Early Warning
- Recommendations
